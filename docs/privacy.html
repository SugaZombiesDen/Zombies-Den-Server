Zombies Den

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Zombies Den (package com.suga.zombiesden, made by the independent developer "Suga") collects, why, and how you can delete it. Zombies Den is made for players aged 18 and over.

The short version

  • You can play single player with no account and nothing leaves your phone.
  • If you make an online account, we store your email address and your username so the name stays yours and you can get it back on a new phone.
  • When you play online co-op, our server briefly handles your IP address to connect you to other players.
  • We do not sell your data, show personalised ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the game or on this web page.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Email addressOnly if you sign in to an online accountTo send you a one-time sign-in code and let you recover your username. We never send marketing emails.
Username and account IDOnly if you create an online accountSo no one else can take your name, and so other players see the correct name in online games.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or payment details.

Who processes the data

We use two service providers to run online features. They handle data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) – stores accounts (email, username) and sends sign-in codes.
  • Render (render.com) – runs the online co-op server that connects players.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS / secure WebSockets). Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where these providers operate.

When you play online, other players in your game can see your username, operator look and calling card.

How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Co-op connection data is kept only while the game is running.
  • Data saved on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account (email, username and account ID) at any time:

  • In the game: Settings > Gameplay > Online account > DELETE ACCOUNT.
  • On the web: Delete your Zombies Den account – no app needed.
  • By email: write to us from the email on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Your username becomes free for others to claim.

Children

Zombies Den contains intense violence and gore and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Big changes (for example, adding ads) will also be announced in the game.
